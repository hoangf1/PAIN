With D1–D4 the cabin frequencies equal NASA's and the structural modes match App. C's list for every mode in the printed pairs, yet NASA's η_r″ follows the coupling with the floor term **subtracted** (η ratios cluster near 0.6, and near 1 at resonance) while App. B's follows it **added** (Phase 44f). The mode files carry the same sign convention on both aircraft and the cabin modes behave the same at the corner on both, so neither explains it. CR-172428 App. A item 2 is the correction that changed this term (`FNR2 = (FQM*(FINS − FINP))**2` with FINP's sign corrected), and CR-3813's earlier runs needed the uncorrected sign (Phase 61). Leading hypothesis for Phase 108: run 10 was computed before item 2 was applied, while its printed field already carries item 3's 4 dB limit. Not concluded; the H1 and H5 printed η_r″ (10 pairs, independent of the field) are the held-out test.
